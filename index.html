DORAEMON

Doraemon is a fictional character in the Japanese manga and anime series of the same name created by Fujiko Fujio, the pen name of writing team Hiroshi Fujimoto and Motoo Abiko. He is a male robotic cat that travels back in time from the 22nd century to aid a preteen boy named Nobita.

Locations

Open Lot

The Open Lot, also known as Empty Lot in the English dub, is one of the recurring locations in the Doraemon series. It majorly appears in many of the manga chapters, episodes, and feature films. lIt is a plain lot with three stacked concrete culverts, sometimes with several planks, a large tree, some bushes and several pipes on the left. Looking at the grasses and the tree at the lot, the Open Lot looks like it hasn't been owned for some time now, although sometimes construction equipment will be placed here temporarily.

This place is an iconic location through out the series as the place where the gang often gather together, as well as the location for Gian's (deadly) concert. Kaminari's House is located on the right side of the area which the boys often throw a ball into his house (and break his window).

Gadgets

Gadgets are wacky devices that have been used in Doraemon series. They vary in their forms, but their purpose remains the same:
help out people in need.

Bamboo Copter

Bamboo Copter or Take-copter is a gadget of 22nd century which is mostly used by humans for flight.

  • Users can use it to fly from one place to another by just thinking of the direction they wanna move in.
  • It can be put anywhere on the body.
  • It can be used to travel long distances for long period of time.

Anywhere Door

The Anywhere Door is one of the most popular and frequently used gadgets in the series. Its prime function is to transport the user to whatever location they desire by walking through it..

  • One needs to say where they want to go going, then they can open the door and go through it to reach their destination.
  • However, it magically knows where the user wants to go.
  • The Anywhere Door's destination limits to the memory of locations it has been implemented, which means it does not record new areas automatically.

Time Machine

Time Machine is a gadget of 22nd Century which was created to do time travel.

  • Time Machine can be used to travel from one dimension to another. It can be used to travel to future and past.
  • It can also be used to travel to several locations existing in the same timeline.
  • One can try to change the incidents happened in the past or going to happen in the future by using this gadget.

Movies

  • Doraemon: Nobita's Little Space Wars 2021

    Doraemon: Nobita's Little Space Wars 2021 (ドラえもん のび太の宇宙小戦争 2021 Nobita no Uchū Ko Sensō 2021) is the 41st installment of Doraemon feature films. The film was planned to be officially released on March 5, 2021 but was postponed on January 29, 2021 due to limitations during the COVID-19 pandemic. The new release date is yet to be announced.

  • Doraemon: Nobita's New Dinosaur

    Doraemon: Nobita's New Dinosaur is the 40th installment of the Doraemon feature films. It was released in Japan on August 7, 2020. A DVD and Blu-Ray version of the movie was released on December 16, 2020 in Japan, and on March 23, 2021 in Hong Kong.

  • Doraemon: Nobita's Chronicle of the Moon Exploration

    Doraemon: Nobita's Chronicle of the Moon Exploration is the 39th installment of the Doraemon feature films. The franchise's 39th film was released in Japan on March 1, 2019. THE GIFT was used as the ending theme song for the movie.

  • Doraemon: Nobita's Treasure Island

    Doraemon: Nobita's Treasure Island is the 38th installment of the Doraemon feature films. The franchise's 38th film was released in Japan in March 2018. This film is based on Robert Louis Stevenson's treasure island novel. Gen Hoshino - Doraemon is the ending theme song.
