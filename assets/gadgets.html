Gadgets

Gadgets are wacky devices that have been used in Doraemon series. They vary in their forms, but their purpose remains the same: help out people in need.

FREQUENTLY USED GADGETS

Anywhere Door

The Anywhere Door is one of the most popular and frequently used gadgets in the series. It basically resembles a pink door that acts as a convenient portal.

Time Machine

Time Machine is a gadget of 22nd Century which was created to do time travel.

Bamboo Copter

Bamboo Copter or Take-copter is a gadget of 22nd century which is mostly used by humans for flight.

A

Air Cannon

It blasts out powerful bursts of air. To use it, you place your hand in it.

Animal Beam

Aim the light on anyone and he/she will turn into an animal.

Anywhere Door

Open this door and you can go anywhere you like!

B

Bamboo Copter

Place it on you and you’ll be able to fly free!

Big Light

Anything will become bigger when it is lit with this.

Blackhole Pen &
Whitehole Pen

Draw a circle with the blackhole pen and make it suck something inside; and it will come out of the circle drawn with the whitehole pen.

C

Come Come Cat

Like a “welcoming cat,” this will bring you customers that you want to your buisness.

D

Doctor's Kit

Put the sensor on the patient, and it will tell you the name of your disease. It’ll give you medicine to cure it too.

Dream Director's Chair

Sit in this and you’ll be able to direct and change what someone else is dreaming about.

Dress Up Camera

Put a picture of clothes or fashon designs that you like in this camera and snap it at the person you want to dress – and he/she will change into the clothes in an instant.

E

Evolution-Degeneration Beam

Reflect something with the evolution beam and it will evolve into something in the future; do the same using the devolution beam and you’ll be able to see what it looked like in the past.

F

Forgotten Stick

Hit a head with this stick and it’ll make the person forget what happened in the last 10 minutes.

G

Guidance Angel

Put this on your hand and ask for advice – and it will tell what would be the best for you. But once you put it on, you can’t remove it on your own.

Gulliver Tunnel

Enter the wide side of the tunnel and go out of the other, and you’ll become mini-sized. Go through it the other way and you’re back to normal.

H

Happiness Cards

Automatically grants wishes of the owner. For a wish granted, a card in the deck disappears. When only the Joker remains, it will bring the owner a tremendous amount of bad luck. Use this once and you are the owner, until somebody else wants it.

Hypnosis Pendulum

You can easily hypnotise any target of your choice.

I

Invisible Cape

Wear this cape and you will become invisible, and no one would see you.

M

Moodmaker Orchestra

Listen to the music this orchestra plays – and it will enhance your mood, making you joyful or tearful depending on the music.

N

N-S Crest

Like magnets, people wearing the same patches will repel, and those wearing the opposite patches will attract.

P

Pass Loop

Put this loop where you want to pass through – you’ll be able to go through any wall.

Pebble Hat

Put this cap on and you’ll be like a pebble on a roadside – no one would notice or care for you.

S

Small Light

Anything will become smaller when it is lit with this.

Shadow-Cutting Scissors

These scissors are for cutting out your shadow. The shadow will work for you at first, but as time goes by it gets smarter – and in 2 hours, it will try to replace you!

Space Exploration Boat

This is your escape spacepod in times of danger, like when the earth is going to explode! This would automatically take you to a planet where people would be able to live.

Super Gloves

Put these on and you’ll be super strong!

T

Teleporting Submarine

A submarine that will allow you to transform from water to water.

Time Belt

This is what you would use when for some reason you can’t use the Time Machine. It can make you move back and forth in time, but you cannot specify a location.

Time Kerchief

Wrap something in this and it will become newer or older. Wrap someone, and they will become younger or older.

Time Mechine

Travel through time, past or present, on this ride. It is connected inside Noby’s desk drawer.

Time TV

See the past or the future with this TV. You can project the image in 3-D too.

Translation Tool

Eat this and you’ll understand any language.

V

Voodoo Camera

Take someone’s picute and its replica doll of that person will come out of the camera immediately. Anything you do to the doll, will refelct to the actual person.

W

Wrestling Killer

Put a dime in and call out a name who you want to trip. The Tripibnator will trip the person three times no matter what. To cancel, out in a dollar.
